Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my hotel booking?

Go to Bookings in the top menu, select your active reservation, and click "Cancel Booking". Check your hotel's policy for cancellation fee details.

When will I receive my booking confirmation?

Confirmation emails are sent automatically within 15 minutes of payment. Make sure to check your spam or junk folder if it doesn't appear in your inbox.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), GCash, Maya, and PayPal.

Can I change my travel dates after booking?

Date modifications depend on the property or airline policy. Visit your Bookings page to request a date change.

Our customer support team is available always.
